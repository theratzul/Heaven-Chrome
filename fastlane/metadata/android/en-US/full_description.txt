Heaven Chrome is a divine time-manipulation action puzzle adventure where the player guides an angelic soul through perilous heavenly realms.

Navigate past demonic hazards, collect Guardian Angels for divine grace, and reach the Pearly Gates to ascend through 20 handcrafted heavenly realms.

Features:
- Divine Time Shift: Tap and hold the Golden Hourglass to bend time into slow motion and maneuver through tight hazards.
- 20 Handcrafted Heavenly Realms: Ascend from the Celestial Gates to the Throne Room of Paradise.
- Guardian Angels: Free the holy cherubs in each realm to gain Faith Score and celestial energy.
- Real-Time Celestial Synthesizer: Polyphonic ethereal hymns that react and pitch-shift dynamically with time manipulation.
- Responsive Touch Controls: Ergonomic virtual D-Pad and sacred action buttons tailored for mobile gameplay.
- Completely Free & Open Source: No advertisements, no trackers, no in-app purchases, and 100% offline gameplay.
